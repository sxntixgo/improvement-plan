# Track 1: Reading Skills

**Duration:** 1 week | **Total:** 18 hours
**Weekly breakdown:** 8 hrs weekdays + 5 hrs Sat + 5 hrs Sun
**Priority:** FOUNDATIONAL (do first)

-----

## Why First?

These meta-learning skills compound across everything else. Learning *how* to read effectively before diving into technical books will:

- 2-3x your reading speed for all subsequent tracks
- Improve comprehension and retention
- Give you frameworks for tackling dense technical material

-----

## Books You Own

|Book              |Author           |Pages|Focus                          |
|------------------|-----------------|-----|-------------------------------|
|How to Read a Book|Adler & Van Doren|~400 |Analytical & syntopical reading|
|Speed Reading     |Kam Knight       |~200 |Practical speed techniques     |

-----

## Reading Strategy

**Read Knight first** (practical techniques), then **Adler** (deeper framework). This lets you apply speed reading techniques while reading Adler.

## Week 1 Schedule

### Days 1-2: Speed Reading (Knight) — 6 hours

**Read the entire book**, but focus especially on:

|Section                                 |Chapters|Priority|Focus                                   |
|----------------------------------------|--------|--------|----------------------------------------|
|**Section I: Pre-Reading**              |1-3     |⭐ HIGH  |Purpose, Preview, Change Styles         |
|**Section II: Speed Reading Techniques**|4-6     |⭐ HIGH  |Space Reading, Chunking, Subvocalization|
|**Section III: Enhancing Techniques**   |7-9     |MEDIUM  |Fixation, Regression, Visual Range      |
|**Section IV: Comprehension**           |10-12   |⭐ HIGH  |Main Points, Topic Sentences, Vocabulary|
|**Section V: Additional Tips**          |13-15   |MEDIUM  |Memory, Visualization, Eye Health       |

**Key techniques to practice immediately:**

- **Space Reading** (Ch 4) - Look at spaces between words, not words themselves
- **Chunking** (Ch 5) - Group words together
- **Reduce Subvocalization** (Ch 6) - Stop "speaking" words in your head
- **Preview** (Ch 2) - Always skim before deep reading

**Day 1 (Mon):** Sections I-II (Chapters 1-6) — 3 hrs
**Day 2 (Tue):** Sections III-V (Chapters 7-15) — 3 hrs

|-------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|

1. What is the book about as a whole?
1. What is being said in detail, and how?
1. Is the book true, in whole or part?
1. What of it? (So what?)

## Key Concepts Summary

### From Knight (Speed Reading)

|Technique                 |What It Is                         |
|--------------------------|-----------------------------------|
|**Purpose**               |Set clear goal before reading      |
|**Preview**               |Skim structure before deep reading |
|**Space Reading**         |Focus on spaces between words      |
|**Chunking**              |Read word groups, not single words |
|**Reduce Subvocalization**|Stop inner voice reading every word|
|**Main Points First**     |Focus on ideas, not details        |

### From Adler (How to Read a Book)

|Level           |When to Use                     |Time Investment|
|----------------|--------------------------------|---------------|
|**Inspectional**|First pass on any book          |15-60 min      |
|**Analytical**  |Important books worth deep study|Hours-days     |
|**Syntopical**  |Researching a topic across books|Days-weeks     |

**The 4 Questions:**

1. What is the book about as a whole?
1. What is being said in detail?
1. Is it true?
1. What of it?
