Start with **systems and habits** books because you're building a 66-week learning system:
- Southwest Airlines Way = culture and consistency
- Score Takes Care of Itself = standards and preparation
- Atomic Habits = daily habits that compound
- High Output Management = systems thinking

### Discipline for the Long Haul (Priority 2)
Around weeks 11-25, you'll be deep in Python Core and AI/ML Security. These books help with **long-term excellence**:
- Legacy = staying excellent for years (All Blacks dynasty)
- Captains Class = championship persistence
- Leaders Eat Last = taking care of yourself
- Start With Why = remembering your purpose

### Mastery & Deep Work (Priority 3)
Weeks 26-40 are Python Advanced and early Go Deep Dive. These books are about **going deep**:
- Bielsa book = obsessive preparation and depth
- Paradigma Guardiola = mastery through evolution
- Creativity Inc = building excellent work
- Elon Musk = pushing limits

## Tips for Success

**Reading Discipline:**
- 📖 Read 20-30 pages every night before bed
- ⏰ Set a consistent time (e.g., 10:00 PM - 10:30 PM)
- 📝 Take notes on key lessons in Obsidian
- 🔄 Re-read favorite chapters when you need motivation

**Integration with Learning:**
- Connect leadership lessons to your technical learning
- Apply "standards of performance" from Walsh to your code
- Use "atomic habits" for daily learning consistency
- Think like Buffett: compound your knowledge over time

**When to Read What:**
- 😴 **Tired?** Read shorter books (Six Thinking Hats, Start With Why)
- 🔥 **Motivated?** Read intense books (Elon Musk, Bielsa)
- 🌙 **Before bed?** Avoid intense books, stick to inspiring but calm
- 📈 **Need motivation?** Re-read favorite chapters from sports books

**Track Your Progress:**
- ✅ Check off books as you complete them
- 📊 Update "Books Completed" count monthly
- 📝 Write blog post reviews for your favorite books
- 🎯 Share key lessons on social media
