## Key Concepts

**From Smart Notes (Ahrens):**

- Fleeting notes → Literature notes → Permanent notes
- Zettelkasten method
- Writing as thinking
- Linking ideas, not filing them

**From Building a Second Brain (Forte):**

- PARA: Projects, Areas, Resources, Archive
- CODE: Capture, Organize, Distill, Express
- Progressive summarization
- Just-in-time vs just-in-case learning
