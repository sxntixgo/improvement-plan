## Time Savings Philosophy

**Reading a technical book:** ~1-2 hours per 50 pages (slower, passive)
**Video course with exercises:** ~1 hour video = 2-3 hours with coding (faster, active)

**Key insight:** For your learning style (project-based), courses often provide FASTER learning because:
- You code along immediately
- Less time reading theory, more time building
- Instant feedback loops
- Built-in projects reduce "what should I build?" paralysis
