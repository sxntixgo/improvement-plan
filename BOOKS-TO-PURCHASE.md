## 🛒 Where to Buy

### Best Prices
1. **O'Reilly Books** (AI Engineering, Asyncio, Learning Go, Programming TypeScript):
   - O'Reilly Learning Platform subscription (~$50/month, cancel after downloading)
   - Or buy individually from O'Reilly.com
   - Often 40% off sales

2. **Pragmatic Bookshelf** (pytest book):
   - PragProg.com (frequent sales)
   - eBook + PDF bundle recommended

3. **Manning** (100 Go Mistakes):
   - Manning.com (frequent "Deal of the Day" 50% off)
   - Subscribe to newsletter for discount codes

4. **Let's Go** (Alex Edwards):
   - https://lets-go.alexedwards.net/
   - Self-published, fixed price

5. **HTB Academy**:
   - https://academy.hackthebox.com/
   - Monthly subscription

### Money-Saving Tips
- ✅ **O'Reilly subscription**: Get 1 month ($50), download all O'Reilly books, cancel
- ✅ **Manning Deal of Day**: Wait for 50% off (frequent sales)
- ✅ **Humble Bundle**: Watch for programming book bundles
- ✅ **Black Friday/Cyber Monday**: Major discounts (Nov 2026)
